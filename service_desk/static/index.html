Why trust it

Every step links to an official source

Language models can sound sure about things that are wrong. Service Desk doesn't ask you to trust the model: the rules below are enforced in code, and every answer shows you where it comes from so you can check it.

Official sites only

Search runs with Tavily restricted to the domains below. Every result is checked again in code, which rejects look-alikes such as agenziaentrate.gov.it.evil.com. If nothing official is found, the model is not called at all.

No source, no step

The model must cite a numbered source for every step, document and deadline. Citations to sources that don't exist are dropped, and anything left without one is removed and listed under “Not covered by official sources”.

Deadlines quoted from your letter

A date from a letter is shown only if it is written in the letter, as 30/11/2026, 30.11.2026 or 30 novembre 2026. You see the sentence it came from, highlighted.

Nothing is stored

Letters are sent to the model for one answer and dropped. Your ticked steps are saved only in this browser.

Allowed sources

Any page on these domains or their subdomains, plus any comune website (comune.<name>.it).
